To obtain an accurate scope confirmation and immediate quote, provide these details when messaging on WhatsApp:

01

Assessment Document

The exact assignment prompt, rubric, lecture slide context, or case study document.

02

Target Word Count

Specify expected word count (e.g. 2,000 words ±10%) or required page length.

03

Submission Deadline

State your deadline date and time (with timezone if outside Bangladesh).

04

Referencing & Data Files

Identify referencing style and attach any raw Excel datasets or SPSS files needed.
