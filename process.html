Detailed Process Steps

01
Phase One

Prepare & send the brief

Gather all pertinent materials for your assignment. Send documents directly to WhatsApp at 01893291245.

Checklist to include:
• Exact submission deadline & timezone
• Target word count or page limit
• Degree level (Undergraduate, Master's) & subject
• Assignment brief, rubric, and required source rules
• Referencing style (APA, Harvard, OSCOLA, etc.)
02
Phase Two

Discuss scope through WhatsApp

Direct dialogue allows swift clarification of question subtleties, dataset readiness, or theoretical frameworks.

We verify whether literature must be restricted to peer-reviewed sources, whether specific corporate case studies are mandatory, and evaluate analytical tool requirements (Excel, Power BI, SPSS).

Phase Three

Confirm arrangement & terms

Decide on the most cost-effective arrangement: the monthly retainer (starting at ৳30) or the fixed outside-assignment word count tier.

04
Phase Four

Complete the agreed support

The writer drafts, researches, calculates, and styles the work according to established university standards and academic style guides.

Includes rigorous reference formatting, calculation validation in spreadsheet models, and structured paragraph progression.

05
Phase Five

Receive structured files

Completed documents are transmitted directly via WhatsApp or email in requested file formats.

Transmitted as Microsoft Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx), Power BI (.pbix), or PDF formatted to your font, spacing, and title-page guidelines.
06
Phase Six

Review & ask relevant questions

Take time to read the delivered drafts thoroughly. Ask clarifying questions regarding literature interpretations, formula workings, or methodology logic.

Ensures you are fully prepared to understand, reference, and use the delivered model in accordance with your university’s academic integrity regulations.
